# Prepared

> Prepared is an AI-powered emergency response platform for 911 communications, now operating as the intelligence layer of Axon 911. Its assistive AI supports call-takers, dispatchers, and supervisors with non-emergency triage, real-time transcription, live translation, multi-channel dispatch monitoring, and quality assurance. Prepared reports supporting 1,000+ PSAPs and protecting 90M+ people. It does not currently publish a public developer API or OpenAPI.
